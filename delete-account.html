Delete your Coinlark data

You can delete everything Coinlark stores about you at any time.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Coinlark and go to Settings.
  2. Under Privacy & data, tap Delete my data and confirm.

This erases your alerts, favorites, floating-panel coins and settings on the device right away. Uninstalling Coinlark removes the same data.

What we store about you

Nothing. Coinlark has no accounts and does not send your data to our servers. Your purchase history is kept by Google under your Google account; reinstalling Coinlark and tapping Restore purchases brings Pro back.

Questions

Email lyrasoftsupport@gmail.com with the subject “Coinlark data deletion” and we will reply within 30 days.
